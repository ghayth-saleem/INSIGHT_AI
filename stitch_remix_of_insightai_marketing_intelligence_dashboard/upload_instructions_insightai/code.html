How to Export Your Data

1

Open Meta Business Suite

Go to business.facebook.com and log in with the account linked to your Instagram page.

2

Navigate to Insights

From the left sidebar, click on Insights to open your performance dashboard.

3

Select the Content Tab

At the top of the Insights page, click the Content tab to see all published posts.

4

Click Export Data

Look for the Export button in the top-right corner of the content table.

5

Choose Range & Format

Select at least 30 days of data, pick CSV as the format, and click Export.

6

Upload to InsightAI

Drag the downloaded CSV file into the upload zone below, or click to browse.
